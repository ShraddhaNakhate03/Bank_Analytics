# Bank Analytics Dashboard 

## Project Overview

The Bank Analytics Dashboard is a business intelligence solution designed to analyze large-scale retail banking datasets and transform fragmented financial records into actionable portfolio insights. The project focuses on credit risk evaluation, borrower behavior analysis, revolving balance trends, and customer verification impact assessment using Excel and Power BI.

---

# STAR Method Explanation

## Situation

Retail banking systems generated massive volumes of customer loan and credit data across disconnected operational platforms. Due to the absence of a centralized analytical framework, risk management teams struggled to:

* Track multi-year borrowing patterns
* Analyze relationships between borrower risk grades and revolving balances
* Identify high-risk customer clusters
* Evaluate repayment performance based on customer verification status
* Generate data-driven insights for portfolio optimization

The lack of integrated reporting limited strategic decision-making for credit risk assessment.

---

## Task

The primary objective of this project was to design and develop an end-to-end banking analytics and business intelligence solution capable of:

* Consolidating and cleaning large financial datasets
* Performing detailed credit risk analysis
* Mapping borrower behavior across multiple dimensions
* Creating interactive dashboards for executive-level reporting
* Supporting risk officers with data-backed portfolio insights

The project required transforming raw banking records into meaningful analytical visuals and performance indicators.

---

## Action

To achieve the project goals, the following steps were implemented:

### Data Cleaning & Preparation

* Used advanced Microsoft Excel functionalities for:

  * Data cleaning
  * Null value handling
  * Outlier detection
  * Data normalization
  * Structural formatting

### Data Analysis in Power BI

* Imported and modeled cleaned datasets in Power BI Desktop
* Developed calculated measures and KPIs using DAX
* Conducted year-over-year loan disbursement trend analysis
* Built matrix visualizations to analyze:

  * Revolving balances
  * Credit grades
  * Sub-grade level borrower patterns

### Risk Segmentation & Comparative Analysis

* Performed borrower segmentation based on:

  * Credit risk grades
  * Loan status
  * Verification categories
* Compared repayment performance between:

  * Verified customers
  * Non-verified customers

### Dashboard Development

Designed an interactive dashboard containing:

* Loan trend analysis
* Credit risk heatmaps
* Revolving balance distribution
* Verification impact reports
* Dynamic filters and slicers for drill-down analysis

---

## Result

The final solution delivered a centralized portfolio analytics platform that transformed complex banking datasets into interactive business intelligence reports.

### Key Outcomes

* Identified multi-year borrowing and loan disbursement trends
* Detected high-risk borrower sub-grade clusters based on revolving credit behavior
* Revealed repayment performance differences between verified and non-verified accounts
* Improved visibility into portfolio risk exposure
* Enabled data-driven decision-making for senior credit risk officers

The dashboard provided a scalable analytical framework for optimizing loan verification strategies and strengthening portfolio management processes.
